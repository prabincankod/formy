## Key findings (Measured, live SERPs)
- Category crowded with new low-DR indie tools (ServerForm, FormHandle, Formboost, Noundry, splitforms, Formz, GetYourForms, FormsList, Form Plume, SubmitKit, onsubmit, W3Forms, FormWit, Form Dump). No giant owns most terms except Formspree/FormBackend/Jotform/Typeform.
- 6+ competitors already rank for "formsubmit alternative" with thin pages — Formy has NO alternative pages (biggest gap).
- "form submission api" SERP is all docs (Gravity Forms, apiforms, splitforms, w3forms, OpnForm) — Formy has no /docs.
- No /pricing page; "formspree pricing" + comparison intent unserved.
- Roundups ranking for "best form backend for developers": formtorch.com (2026-04), orbitforms.ai (2026-03), formgrid.dev — Formy not listed.

## Decisions / next actions
- Build /alternatives/{formsubmit,formspree,formbackend,formkeep} + hub first (P0 quick wins).
- Add /docs (API ref) + /webhooks + /pricing.
- Rewrite 3 existing blog posts + 2 use-case pages with aaron content-writer (CORE-EEAT).
- Interlink clusters, update app/sitemap.ts after first 4 pages, submit roundup outreach.
